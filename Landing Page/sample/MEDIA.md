# Demo media

All people, names, dialogue and events in this demo are fictional. The photos were generated specifically for this demo with Gemini via OpenRouter. No photos, video, private messages, or biographical details from the private reference website were reused or submitted to image generation.

Seven photographic images were generated using one fictional adult couple as a visual reference for continuity. The generation record and prompts are in image-provenance.json. The API-reported total for these seven generations was USD 0.274647. No API credential is included in the demo or its source files.

Music choices point to official YouTube uploads:

- Chiké — Roju: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19WTWVw15n0
- Johnny Drille — How Are You (My Friend): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CSK5eApRGwQ
- Ben Platt — Grow As We Go: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aDeNQNtW1f8

Titles and channel identities were checked against YouTube oEmbed. These are streaming embeds, not audio downloads. They require internet access and may be affected by browser policies or platform restrictions. This does not grant permission to include those recordings in an exported MP4. The optional local-audio feature is for a recording the viewer is permitted to use; it stays in the browser and is not uploaded.

The earlier illustrated demo is preserved separately in the sibling For Zara directory. Its synthetic score is not the soundtrack of this photographic rebuild.
