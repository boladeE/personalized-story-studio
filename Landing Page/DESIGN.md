# Little Things — landing direction

Working brand, not a confirmed company name. A Decide/Learn surface: help a Nigerian gift buyer understand the offer, see a real sample, and begin a brief.

## Design tokens

- Petal #f5eaf1 — quiet main canvas, not generic warm cream.
- Aubergine #2e2030 — typography and deep-background sections.
- Mulberry #66354d — primary actions.
- Lilac #d9c9e5 — contrasting format canvas.
- Chalk #fffafc — papers and bright text.
- Fern #dce4d4 — selected occasion chips and small counterpoint.

Bodoni Moda for a deliberately personal, slightly theatrical display voice; DM Sans for calm, practical customer information. Both hosted locally with font licenses. One large headline, not isolated italic accent words.

## Composition

Hero: left-aligned confident promise; on the right, an oversized photographic keepsake with a smaller overlapping photo, a handwritten-style recipient inscription, and one tactile circular sample link. Photographs are previously generated fictional media, disclosed nearby. The photo composition, not repeated scroll effects, carries the personality.

Then a wide real sample with its own cinematic palette; two unequal format panels (interactive site / downloadable film); a simple three-step process; pilot price and scope note; FAQ; one closing invitation. No fake reviews, invented customer counts, payment claims, WhatsApp funnel, or guaranteed delivery promises.

## Review against brief

Avoid another version of the dark gift itself as the entire storefront: the buyer needs clarity, not a second private screening. Keep the dark cinema sample as a contrast within a quieter petal/lilac storefront. Do not use repeated SaaS cards or a feature-icon grid. Real-photo artifacts must remain central. The working name and pilot price remain editable.

The brief flow is local-only: validation, readable review, and a downloadable text brief. No uploads, payment collection, email sending, or fabricated order confirmation. State this before the form is used.
