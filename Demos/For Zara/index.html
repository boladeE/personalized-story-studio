For Zara, from Tobi

You were “five minutes away”. You arrived with an apology, a yellow umbrella, and a very serious opinion about puff-puff. I was glad I waited.

Our kind of ordinary: the long way home, with one last song; Saturday puff-puff runs, extra pepper, obviously; and calls that outlived the battery.

The things I keep on repeat: your laugh at your own jokes, the way you notice the quiet people, and your very serious snack opinions.

Zara, you make a Tuesday feel like a plan. You remember the small things. And somehow, even the wrong turns end up being my favourite part. So today, let me make a little fuss. For your laugh. Your heart. For you.

Happy birthday, Zara. More little things. More you. Always, Tobi.
